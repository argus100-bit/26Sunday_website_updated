## `/company/about`

### Section 1
**Headline:** "We believe that precision is the respect for reality."

**Body:**
"26Sunday did not begin with a product. First, there was a question: why is it so hard to build trust, and why do the tools that are meant to do that make things so hard?

We saw good businesses struggle with systems that demand too much and give too little. That's why we set out to make software and services that work the way people really think, not the way by going through pages of a user manual.

At our core, we believe that the most powerful enterprise tools are the ones that feel effortless. Every idea and its execution is thoughtfully curated with no compromise to clarity."

### Section 2 — Our Mission
**Headline:** "Make trust the easiest thing for a business to prove."

**Body:**
"Governance, Risk and Compliance has always been one of those areas where the stakes are high, but the tools feel like they're working against you. Often buried in spreadsheets, scattered questionnaires, endless back-and-forth, and processes that slow everyone down at the time when speed matters most.

That's why we created 26Sunday.

Our GRC platform combines four closely related solutions:
Trust Center, Questionnaire, Readiness Assessment, and Status Page - and is driven by 26Sunday AI, so your team can get an efficient, complete, real-time picture of your compliance posture without the mess. All the insights connected. Making all workflows simple."

**Headline:** "But we were not done."

**Body:**
"For teams who want to move even faster, 26Sunday SaaS+ puts our in-house GRC analysts to work side by side with you. These are real experts who use the platform as it was meant to be used, doing the heavy lifting so all you have to do is review and approve. Less time wasted on the details. More time to make the decisions that matter.

We're not building software. We are building the infrastructure of trust for businesses that cannot afford to get it wrong and have no time to waste.

Precision. Transparency. Trust. That is 26Sunday."
