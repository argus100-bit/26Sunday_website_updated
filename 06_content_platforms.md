## `/platform` (comparison page)

### Comparison table — use `<ComparisonTable />`

| | SaaS | SaaS+ |
|---|---|---|
| **Core engine** | AI-powered automation | AI-powered automation, backed by certified experts |
| **Best for** | Teams ready to self-serve and move fast | Teams who want expert judgment on the harder calls |
| **Trust Center setup** | AI builds the foundation; your team completes and maintains the rest | Built end-to-end by our team, fully populated and maintained for you |
| **Questionnaires** | AI drafts answers from your Knowledge Base or Trust Center, then your team reviews and sends | AI drafts answers from your Knowledge Base or Trust Center, then a security expert reviews, refines, and signs off |
| **Readiness Assessment** | AI generates a gap checklist; your team works through the fixes internally | AI generates the checklist, and our experts work through the fixes on your behalf |
| **Ideal when** | Your team has the bandwidth to execute on AI-generated guidance | Stakes are high, deals are complex, and you need certified expertise on demand |

**Closing statement below table:**
"Both platforms use the same AI engine and the same trust data. SaaS+ does not replace SaaS, it adds a layer of human expertise on top of it. You're not choosing a 'better' platform, you're choosing how much human judgment you want in your workflow."

### FAQ — use `<FAQAccordion />`

**Q: What's the difference between SaaS and SaaS+?**
A: SaaS is our AI-powered platform that consists of Trust Center, Questionnaire, Status Page, and Readiness Assessment, all self-serve. SaaS+ adds certified security experts on top of that same platform, who build and maintain your Trust Center, complete questionnaire items AI leaves unanswered, and work through your readiness checklist with you.

**Q: Can I switch from SaaS to SaaS+ later?**
A: Yes. SaaS+ doesn't replace your existing infrastructure. Rather, it adds a layer of human expertise on top of the same AI engine that you are already using so upgrading does not mean starting over.

**Q: Which compliance frameworks does 26Sunday support?**
A: 26Sunday is compatible with large frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, etc. You can choose any framework in Readiness Assessment and the platform will do the gap analysis for that particular framework.

**Q: How does the AI know what to answer in questionnaires?**
A: The AI retrieves information directly from your Trust Center and knowledge base, and does not use generic templates, so every answer is based on your actual policies and controls.

**Q: What happens if AI can't answer a questionnaire item?**
A: On SaaS, unanswered items are flagged for your team to complete. On SaaS+, our certified experts pick up exactly where AI leaves off and complete those items on your behalf.

**Q: Is my data secure on 26Sunday?**
A: Yes. Your trust and compliance data stays within your knowledge base and is used only to power your own Trust Center, questionnaire responses, and readiness tracking — never shared across customers.

**Q: What's the SLA for support requests?**
A: SaaS+ customers get 24/7 human support with a guaranteed 6-hour response window. SaaS customers receive platform support during business hours.
